GitOps for marketing_

Your campaigns, budgets and ads, with their copy and images, written down as config files your team reviews like code. Every change is proposed, discussed and approved before it reaches an ad platform, and the history of your config files is the history of your accounts.

# how it works

How it works

The same loop software teams use for infrastructure, applied to your ad accounts.

  1. 01

    Draft

    Write down the state you want: the campaigns, their budgets, targeting and creatives.

    campaign.yaml
  2. 02

    Change

    A change arrives as a proposal, from a person or from the agency, showing exactly what would change.

    - daily_budget: 80 + daily_budget: 50
  3. 03

    Review

    Your team comments, asks for changes and approves, with every check visible.

    approved · checks passed
  4. 04

    Apply

    Once approved, mops brings the ad platforms in line with the config files.

    mops ads apply

edit / draft → review / checks → merge / apply → analyse reports → next draft

# what mops manages

Five parts of an ad account

  • LinkedIn

    Accounts

    LinkedIn Ads, linked in the agency's setup.

  • LinkedIn Ads

    Campaigns

    Campaign groups, campaigns and their single-image, text and promoted-post ads, with targeting and budgets, compared with what is live.

  • Planned

    Audiences

    Who you reach, described once and used across campaigns.

  • Images and copy

    Assets

    Copy and images, generated or added by hand, versioned with the campaigns that use them.

  • In the agency

    Reports

    Daily spend, impressions, clicks and conversions, read live from LinkedIn for the agency's dashboard.

# agents

AI proposes, people approve

mops builds the campaign from your brief, and AI writes its copy and banner. When a reviewer comments on the copy or the banner, AI rewrites its prompt and adds a version with new candidates. It never approves, merges or changes an account on its own.

  • AI writes the copy and banner of a campaign drafted from a brief
  • AI rewrites the copy or banner prompt from each review comment on it
  • you choose candidates, resolve comments, approve and merge

# dashboard

See what each change did

The agency plots daily spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and CPC for each account, campaign group and campaign, with a marker for every merged proposal. Under the plot are the active campaigns, the open proposals and the history of what each merge changed.

# history

Your account history is your Git history

Every change is a reviewed commit with its reasons attached. To undo a change, revert it: the next apply brings the platforms back in line.

# cli

The same workflow from the command line

Developers will run the same workflow with the mops CLI and library: pull an account into config files, draft from a brief, plan and apply. The CLI is not released yet.
