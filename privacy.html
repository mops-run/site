Privacy policy

Updated 24 November 2026

mops is run by mops Ltd, registered in the United Kingdom, at Dream Hunter, South Dock Marina, Rope Street, London, SE16 7SZ. Write to run@mops.run about your data.

What we collect

  • The Tell me more form: your email address. Customer.io keeps it in its EU data centre and sends you emails about mops from run@mops.run. Each email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Visits, only after you accept: the pages you open, the link that brought you here with its UTM tags and ad click IDs, and the page you came from, recorded by Customer.io in its EU data centre. Its script stores an ID in cookies and local storage. After you accept, the form also links your visits to your email address.
  • Your choice: your browser keeps Accept or Reject in its local storage, and nothing is sent. To choose again, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Fonts: the page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages serves the site and receives your IP address.

Why

To answer the people who ask about mops, and, with your consent, to see which links bring people to mops.

How long

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to run@mops.run. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk.
